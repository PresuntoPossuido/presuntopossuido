PresuntoBot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

PresuntoBot is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload clips of the owner’s own live streams to the owner’s TikTok account as drafts.

Data accessed: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to identify the connected account, and permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

Storage: the access token is stored only on the owner’s personal computer. No data is stored on external servers.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

Third-party users: the tool does not collect data from any other TikTok users.

Deletion: access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (manage app permissions), and the locally stored token can be deleted by the owner.
